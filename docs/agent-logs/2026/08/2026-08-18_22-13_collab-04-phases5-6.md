## User Journeys

| # | Persona | Journey | Acceptance criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | Space owner | Creates and renames a space | Creator is the sole owner; all members observe the new name after their membership version advances. |
| U2 | Space owner | Invites an editor by email, revokes/resends an invite | Email is normalized, raw tokens are emailed but only hashes persist, expiry is seven days, revoke invalidates the old token, and resend issues a different single-use token. |
| U3 | Invited editor | Accepts an invitation | Only the authenticated account with the matching normalized email can accept; expired, revoked, or reused tokens reveal no space/member details and cannot add membership. |
| U4 | Space owner | Removes an editor or transfers ownership | Removal revokes HTTP/WebSocket access immediately; transfer atomically leaves exactly one owner and makes the former owner an editor. |
| U5 | Space editor | Leaves a space | Self-removal succeeds for editors, fails for owners, advances membership version, unsubscribes live clients, and purges the cooperating client's active local replica/queue. |
| U6 | Space owner | Requests deletion | The space becomes inaccessible immediately, all members refresh/revoke, data remains during the 30-day retention window, and only the background purge removes its database/uploads/metadata after the deadline. |
| U7 | Account owner | Attempts account deletion while owning spaces | Deletion is blocked until every owned space is transferred or requested for deletion; membership cleanup never leaves a space without exactly one owner. |
| U8 | Space member | Manages images in a shared Document | Every image route resolves the qualified database and authorizes membership, including query-token fetch; non-members receive a non-disclosing 404 and responses are private/no-store. |
| U9 | Space member | Views/restores shared Document revisions | Qualified routes authorize membership and actor display is derived from backend identity, never client fields or replica site IDs. |
| U10 | Editor | Transfers a Document personal↔space or space↔space | A durable checkpoint precedes copying; canonical same-origin Markdown image destinations are rewritten, missing/noncanonical images remain with visible warnings, destination rows/bytes are verified before optional source deletion, and the UI states that revision history remains at the source. |
| U11 | Editor recovering a transfer | Encounters a mid-transfer failure | A visible duplicate remains and can resume, keep both, or delete the source without creating a period where neither copy exists. |
| U12 | Operator | Backs up/restores shared spaces | `_spaces.db`, space databases, and matching uploads share a manifest/snapshot set; restore stages and validates integrity, containment, invariants, and completeness before replacement. |
| U13 | Collaboration spike tester | Edits two OverType textareas bound to one `Y.Text` | Insert/replace/paste/composition/selection/origin-scoped undo pass without full-document overwrite. |
| U14 | Collaboration spike tester | Crashes after an acknowledged update and reconnects with one unacknowledged update | Acknowledged state restores durably and only the unacknowledged update replays; failure stops COLLAB-05 implementation. |
